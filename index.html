About me

I'm an energy systems modeler with industry and research experience spanning 2017–2024, and my work has always circled one question: how do we plan energy systems that are genuinely sustainable? I develop linear-optimization energy models (TEMOA, OSeMOSYS, CLEWs) in Python — including cost-optimization and production-cost analysis — and combine them with GIS spatial analysis and data work to turn model outputs into decisions people can act on. I've worked on long-term sustainability pathways for Canada's Atlantic provinces, and on the land-use questions that come with high renewable penetration. I also teach and present this work — I like the part where a model stops being a model and starts being a conversation.
